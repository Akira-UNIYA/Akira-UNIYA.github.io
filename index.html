Profile

2026年、神戸大学大学院経営学研究科博士前期課程を修了。現在は同研究科の博士後期課程に在籍。専門はイノベーション、製品開発、生産管理。組織学会、工業経営研究学会、日本経営学会の会員。 Completed Master's degree in 2026 and currently enrolled in the Ph.D. program at the Graduate School of Business Administration, Kobe University. Specializes in innovation, product development, and production management. Member of the Organization Science Society of Japan, Japan Society for Industrial Management, and Japan Academy of Business Administration.
